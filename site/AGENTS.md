# Website scope: private research inventory

This site is a private informational inventory for research-use-only peptide
products currently owned by the operator. The products must not be represented
as FDA-approved medications or as approved for human use.

Display only owner-reviewed product names, confirmed vial sizes or packaging,
factual inventory status, optional informational pricing when applicable, and
factual research information with traceable provenance. Pricing and inventory
status are informational only; they must not imply a sale, guaranteed
availability, allocation, eligibility, safety, quality outcome, or commitment.

Do not provide medical advice, recommend that anyone take a product, make
disease-treatment or efficacy claims, or provide dosing, mixing, preparation,
reconstitution, route, administration, or other human-use instructions. Never
derive public content from private mixing/dosing reference files.

Organize the website around a prominent research-only notice, searchable
catalog, concise product records, confirmed vial/package details, status,
optional price, and source/review metadata. Keep the layout readable and
mobile-first with high contrast and generous touch targets. Do not add cart,
checkout, buy/order/reserve actions, payment capture, quantity selection,
urgency messaging, testimonials, or marketing claims. Keep the site private
unless separate owner approval authorizes reviewed publication and the required
privacy, legal, host, and security checks.
