# Content Remains Within the Documented Research-Catalog Scope

## Decision

Keep all public content within the documented research-catalog boundary. Exclude
dosing, mixing, preparation, administration, therapeutic or personal-use
guidance, testimonials, promotional equivalence claims, and accessories or
tools that facilitate human administration.

## Rationale

The scope is a neutral catalog and a request for owner contact, not health or
personal-use education. FDA warning material illustrates the relevance of
human-use claims and dosing instructions in its review of online offerings.

## Consequences

- Review applies to visible copy, images, alt text, metadata, structured data,
  FAQs, confirmation messages, and downloadable materials.
- Content checks can flag terms for review but cannot replace human context
  review.
- Scientific descriptions must be accurate, sourced, and free of claims that
  imply a product is equivalent to an approved medicine.

## Evidence considered

- [FDA warning letter to Xcel Research LLC](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/xcel-research-llc-694608-12102024)
- [FDA's concerns with unapproved GLP-1 drugs](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fdas-concerns-unapproved-glp-1-drugs-used-weight-loss)
